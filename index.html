01

DHAROHAR

Design of Domestic Interior and Landscaping

Type Cultural heritage with modern comfort
Area 2700 sq. ft
Location Dehradun, India
Software AutoCAD / SketchUp / Enscape
01

Project Overview

The objective was to transform a single-storied residential structure in Dehradun into a personalized "home" for a family of four. The design process emphasized a systematic, research-driven approach to move from general aspirations to a high-fidelity domestic environment.

02

Concept

The design philosophy bridges traditional Indian aesthetics with modern functionalism. The space is characterized by intricate patterns, warm earthy tones, and a robust use of natural materials like wood and stone.

03

Moodboard

The mood board reflects the essence of traditional Rajasthani architecture blended with contemporary living. Warm earthy tones like beige, terracotta, deep blue, and natural wood create a rich and inviting atmosphere.

04

Material Board

The material palette focuses on natural and traditional finishes. Patterned ceramic tiles, marble surfaces, and sandstone textures add cultural character, while warm wooden finishes are used in furniture and cabinetry. Decorative wallpapers, metal accents, and soft fabrics balance the traditional materials with a refined and contemporary aesthetic.

05

Planning

The layout is organized into private, semi-private, and public zones — including master bedroom, child bedrooms, guest room, dining, kitchen, living room, and drawing room.

05

WALL-MOUNTED FOLDING STUDY TABLE

Stacking & Folding Furniture

Area 120 sq. ft
Software AutoCAD / SketchUp / Enscape
01

Problem Statement

The existing study table blocked circulation and occupied unnecessary space within a compact room layout, creating movement restrictions and safety issues.

02

Key Issues

Narrow circulation path
Collision risk with table edges
Poor space utilization
No flexibility in furniture usage
03

Design Aim

"To create a flexible and ergonomic workspace that maximizes space efficiency."

04

Research & Design Development

A wall-mounted folding study table integrated with storage that transforms from a compact vertical panel into a functional horizontal workspace. Design features: Foldable mechanism, integrated storage, minimal floor obstruction, compact modern form.

05

Mechanism & Ergonomics

Mechanism Components: Butt hinges (rotational movement), Support brackets (structural support), Magnetic lock (secure closure), Wall frame (stability).

Transformation: Closed Position → Compact Panel | Open Position → Ergonomic Workspace

Ergonomic Dimensions: Height: 70–75 cm, Width: 80–100 cm, Depth: 45–60 cm, Knee clearance: ≥ 60 cm

Materials: MDF / plywood, Stainless steel hinges, Laminate finish

06

Final Outcome & Conclusion

"The project demonstrates how transformable furniture systems can improve flexibility, safety, and spatial efficiency in compact residential interiors."
